# Finora – Multilingual AI Financial Planning Assistant

## Problem
Most financial planning tools are:
- In English or complex language  
- Hard for first‑time users to understand  
- Not personalized to individual goals and contexts  

This is especially true for students, young professionals, and first‑time earners in India who think better in their regional language.

## Solution
**Finora** is an AI‑powered, multilingual financial assistant that:
- Collects basic financial data from the user  
- Evaluates their financial health, expenses, and saving status  
- Generates a **detailed roadmap** for financial planning  
- Suggests **smart money hacks**, **side hustle ideas**, and **finance courses**  
- Explains everything in the user’s **chosen language**

## Key Features

### 1. Financial Health Check
- Analyzes income, expenses, and savings  
- Shows a clear picture of current financial health  
- Highlights areas of concern (e.g., high discretionary spend, low savings rate)

### 2. Expense & Saving Status
- Breaks down expenses by category  
- Compares spending vs. ideal benchmarks  
- Shows saving rate and progress towards goals

### 3. Detailed Financial Planning Roadmap
- Short‑term actions (next 1–3 months)  
- Mid‑term plan (3–12 months)  
- Long‑term strategy (1–5 years)  
- Goal‑based planning (emergency fund, higher education, travel, etc.)

### 4. Smart Money Hacks
- Practical tips to reduce unnecessary spend  
- Simple rules for budgeting and saving  
- Behavior‑change nudges in local language

### 5. Side Hustle Suggestions
- Personalized side income ideas based on skills, time, and risk appetite  
- Rough earning potential and effort level  
- How to get started (first 3 steps)

### 6. Finance Knowledge & Courses
- Curated list of beginner‑friendly finance courses and resources  
- Content matched to user level (student, first job, etc.)  
- Options in multiple languages where available

## How It Works (User Flow)

1. **Language Selection**  
   User chooses their preferred language (English, Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati).

2. **Data Input**  
   User enters:
   - Monthly income  
   - Major expense categories  
   - Financial goals and constraints  

3. **AI Analysis**  
   Finora evaluates:
   - Financial health score  
   - Expense and saving status  
   - Risk profile and goals  

4. **Personalized Output**  
   User sees:
   - Financial health dashboard  
   - Detailed roadmap for financial planning  
   - Smart money hacks  
   - Side hustle suggestions  
   - Recommended finance courses
